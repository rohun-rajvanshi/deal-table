# How to play

Be the first player to collect **3 complete property sets in different colours**.

These are the official Monopoly Deal rules (2008 Hasbro rulebook) as this table plays them, plus how to make each move on screen.

## Starting a game

| Mode | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Same device** | Choose 2–5 players, type names and tap **Deal the cards**. Between turns a "Pass to…" screen hides the hands. Tap **I'm *name* — show my cards** when the right person has the device. |
| **Online room** | Enter your name and tap **Create room**. Share the 6-letter code. Friends open the site, enter their name and the code and tap **Join**. The host taps **Start game** (2–5 players). |

Each player is dealt 5 cards.

## Your turn

1. **Draw 2 cards.** This happens automatically. If your hand is empty you draw 5.
2. **Play up to 3 cards.** Tap a card in your hand, then choose what to do with it in the action bar. The three dots next to the player's name show how many plays are left.
3. **End your turn.** Tap **End turn** to stop early. After your 3rd play the turn ends by itself a moment later. If you have more than 7 cards you'll be asked to discard down to 7 first.

Moving or flipping a **wild property** that's already on your table is free. Tap it and pick a colour, as often as you like during your turn.

## The three ways to play a card

- **Bank it:** money cards, and action cards if you'd rather keep them as cash. Banked cards are used to pay debts.
- **Lay a property:** properties and wilds go on your table in colour sets.
- **Use an action:** it then goes to the discard pile, except Houses and Hotels, which sit on a set.

## Paying

When you owe money, tap cards on **your own table**: bank cards, buildings or properties. Then tap **Pay**.

- You never pay from your hand.
- There's no change. Overpaying is your choice.
- If everything you have is worth less than you owe, you pay it all automatically. If you have nothing, you pay nothing.
- The rainbow (any-colour) wild has no cash value and can't be used to pay.
- Properties you receive go onto your table. Buildings you receive go into your bank.

## Action cards

| Card | Count | Value | What it does | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sly Deal** | 3 | 3M | Steal one property that isn't part of a full set | Tap a glowing card on an opponent's table → **Steal it** |
| **Forced Deal** | 3 | 3M | Swap one of your properties for one of theirs. Theirs can't be part of a full set; yours can be any. | Tap their card, then one of yours → **Swap** |
| **Deal Breaker** | 2 | 5M | Steal a complete set, including its House and Hotel | Tap an opponent's complete set → **Break the deal** |
| **Just Say No** | 3 | 4M | Cancel an action played against you. Doesn't use a play. | Offered automatically when an action targets you |
| **Debt Collector** | 3 | 3M | One player pays you 5M | Tap an opponent → **Collect 5M** |
| **It's My Birthday** | 3 | 2M | Every other player pays you 2M | Plays immediately |
| **Pass Go** | 10 | 1M | Draw 2 extra cards | Plays immediately |
| **Double The Rent** | 2 | 1M | Played together with a Rent card to double it. Uses a play of its own. | Tap **×2 Double** on the rent screen |
| **House** | 3 | 3M | +3M rent on a full set (not Railroads/Utilities) | Tap your complete set → **Build** |
| **Hotel** | 2 | 4M | +4M rent on a full set that already has a House | Tap your complete set → **Build** |

## Rent cards

| Card | Count | Value | Who pays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two-colour Rent (Brown/Light Blue, Pink/Orange, Red/Yellow, Green/Dark Blue, Railroad/Utility) | 2 of each | 1M | **Every** other player |
| Wild Rent (any colour) | 3 | 3M | **One** player you choose |

Tap **Charge rent**, tap one of your glowing sets, choose a player for Wild Rent, then tap **Charge**. Rent depends on how many cards of that colour you have, up to a full set. You can't charge rent on a colour that only has a rainbow wild.

## Just Say No chains

1. An action hits you and you hold a Just Say No. You're asked: **Just Say No** or **Accept / Pay**.
2. If you say no, the attacker is asked whether to counter with their own Just Say No.
3. If they counter, it's your turn to decide again, and so on until someone stops or runs out.

With Birthday and two-colour Rent, each player decides separately. A Just Say No only protects the player who plays it. Cancelling a doubled rent cancels the Double The Rent too.

## Property sets

| Colour | Cards for a set | Rent (1 → full set) | Card value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brown | 2 | 1, 2 | 1M |
| Light Blue | 3 | 1, 2, 3 | 1M |
| Pink | 3 | 1, 2, 4 | 2M |
| Orange | 3 | 1, 3, 5 | 2M |
| Red | 3 | 2, 3, 6 | 3M |
| Yellow | 3 | 2, 4, 6 | 3M |
| Green | 3 | 2, 4, 7 | 4M |
| Dark Blue | 2 | 3, 8 | 4M |
| Railroad | 4 | 1, 2, 3, 4 | 2M |
| Utility | 2 | 1, 2 | 2M |

**Wild properties** (11): Light Blue/Brown (1M), Light Blue/Railroad (4M), Pink/Orange ×2 (2M), Red/Yellow ×2 (3M), Dark Blue/Green (4M), Green/Railroad (4M), Railroad/Utility (2M), and 2 rainbow wilds that can be any colour (no value).

If you collect more cards of a colour than a set needs, the extras start a second set. Those extras can be stolen with Sly Deal.

## Winning

The game ends as soon as the player whose turn it is has 3 complete sets of different colours. Two sets of the same colour count once.

## Top bar

- **💬 Chat:** online rooms only.
- **Rules:** a quick summary in the app.
- **↻ Restart:** a fresh deal with the same players. In online rooms only the host can restart.
- **🚪 Leave game / Quit game:** back to the home screen. After a refresh, the home screen offers **Rejoin** for an online room you were in.
